# Feedback & Issues

If you encounter any bugs, issues, or have feature requests, please submit your feedback below.

Your submission is **sent to the maintainer for review first**, and is only published to our **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Gdxz-Linus/smartdns-edge/issues)** once confirmed. This keeps the public tracker useful and free of unrelated noise.

- After submitting you will see a "received" message and **no issue number yet** — the report is still under review at that point;
- If we need to follow up, we may reply via the contact details you provide;
- Please **do not** include secrets such as `api-token` or private keys in your message.
